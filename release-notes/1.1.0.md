## Petal 1.1.0

- **Copy Text (OCR):** read Thai and English from photos, screenshots and scanned PDFs, on your Mac.
- **Cut Out:** remove photo backgrounds in one drop.
- **PDF Tools:** password lock / unlock, watermark, page numbers and signatures. Undo in the page organizer.
- **GIF / Video:** turn images into an animated GIF or a slideshow video; compress videos.
- **Batch rename** in the Workspace (`Trip {n}`, `{date}`, …).
- **Automation:** watch folders, ⌥⌘P for files selected in Finder, Finder Quick Actions and `petal://` links for Shortcuts.
- **Folder access** in one place (welcome window and Settings), kept across updates.
- **Thai language** (Settings → Language) and a cleaner menu bar panel with Update and Quit buttons.
- A smaller ring that only shows tools that fit the file.
